## Checked and not a gap (mstack equal or stronger)

- Isolation and merge: m-dispatch (worktree per writer, one file per worker) is at least as strict as OMC team.
- Provenance marks: m-handoff `(done)` / `(verified)` / `(assumed)` has no OMC equivalent.
- Cross-model verification: m-verify's second-model check matches verifier-plus-critic intent.
- Bounded retries: m-dispatch and m-debug already stop after repeated failure, so OMC's team fix-loop cap adds nothing new.
- Notepad or project-memory tiers: OMC's priority/working/manual notepad is covered by mstack's file-based `.mstack/` state.

## Top recommendations

1. Make m-verify baseline-aware (row 1) and wire m-goal's final gate to m-deslop, m-verify and m-review (row 2). This stops "done" claims on red repos and closes the deslop gap.
2. Move m-goal's loop control into `.mstack/goals/<slug>.state.json` with signature and budget counters (row 3), and add a staleness check to m-start (row 5).
3. Ship an optional PreCompact hook for m-handoff (row 4). Real use (49 /compact in the prompt history) makes this the highest-value hook.
4. Add intent and standards axes to m-review (row 6) and the amend-don't-edit rule to m-goal (row 9).
5. Keep m-think's batch questions and m-plan's single critic pass; add the scoring and pre-mortem as light gates, not OMC's full loops.
